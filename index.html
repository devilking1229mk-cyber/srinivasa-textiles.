The Founder's Promise

"Every thread of silk we weave carries the sanctity of our family's blessing."

"When I founded Srinivasa Textiles in 1978, our singular goal was to ensure that when a family dresses for a wedding or milestone festival, every member—from the grandparent in pure mulberry silk to the newborn in organic muslin—experiences supreme purity and scratch-free comfort."

Under Shri Srinivasa's stewardship, over 10,000 artisan weaver families have been supported across Kanchipuram and Arani. Every silk saree is tested for certified SMOI Silk Mark silver-tested zari purity.

About Srinivasa Textiles

The Sacred Craft of Kanchipuram Silk

Since 1978, the master weavers of Srinivasa Textiles have preserved ancient pit-loom techniques. Each heirloom saree takes up to 28 days of meticulous craftsmanship using 3-ply twisted mulberry silk yarns and genuine silver-electroplated tested gold zari.

Our family matching ensembles are dyed together in unified ritual dye baths, guaranteeing 100% color fidelity across men's, women's, and children's festive attire.

🏛️ Silk Mark SMOI Certified Pure mulberry silk purity guaranteed by central silk board.
🛡️ Soft Cotton Inner Linings Zero itchiness or zari scratching for babies and children.
