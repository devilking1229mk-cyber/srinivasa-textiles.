# Full-Family Textile E-Commerce Website Architectural Blueprint & Technical Specifications

A multi-page architecture blueprint and implementation guide designed for web developers and UI/UX designers to build a high-performance family textile web platform.

---

## Executive Summary & Core Architectural Direction

This specification defines a **Multi-Page Website Architecture** designed for high brand authority and effortless user navigation. 

### Core Layout Principles:
1. **Dedicated Homepage (Brand Introduction Only):** The landing page strictly highlights brand story, heritage, physical store establishment photos, and company legacy. No shop catalogs or product grids are shown on the main landing page.
2. **Dedicated Collection & Shopping Hub:** All product displays (Infants, Kids, Women, Men, Fabrics) are moved to a separate multi-department shopping environment accessible via an explicit **"Explore Collections"** CTA button.
3. **Dedicated Owner & Business Operations Dashboard:** Managed on an isolated, secure sub-domain / path (`/admin` or `admin.yourtextile.com`).
4. **Moderate & Compact Media Containers:** Banned oversized / full-bleed image displays on inner department pages. All product and thumbnail containers use medium-to-compact ratios to maximize screen space efficiency.
5. **Strict Element Alignment Standards:** Mandatory vertical and horizontal grid alignment across all sections, footers, headers, cards, and admin widgets.

## 1. Global UI/UX Standards & Interaction Design

### 1.1 Header Navigation & Brand Identity Rule
* **Sticky Brand Header:** Positioned across all pages (Light & Dark modes).
* **Brand Logo / Name Interaction:**
  * **RULE:** Clicking or tapping the store name / logo in the header MUST ALWAYS navigate the user directly back to the **Main Homepage (`/`)** from any section or sub-page.

### 1.2 Theme & Palette Management (Light & Dark Modes)
* **Header Toggle Control:** Universal switch to toggle between Light and Dark themes seamlessly without page reload.
* **Light Mode:**
  * Background: `#FAF8F5` (Warm Cream / Crisp Off-White)
  * Surface Cards: `#FFFFFF` with light grey borders (`#E5E5E5`)
  * Primary Text: `#1A1A1A` (Charcoal Black)
  * Accent: `#0F5257` (Deep Emerald) / `#C5A059` (Warm Gold)
* **Dark Mode:**
  * Background: `#121212` (Deep Charcoal Black)
  * Surface Cards: `#1E1E1E` with subtle slate borders (`#2A2A2A`)
  * Primary Text: `#F0F0F0` (Off-White)
  * Accent: `#56C596` (Mint Emerald) / `#E5C158` (Soft Gold)

### 1.3 Media Display & Image Sizing Guidelines
* **Image Sizing Requirement (Strict Standard):**
  * **BANNED:** Oversized hero product cards, full-screen vertical image loops on catalog pages, and giant zoom frames.
  * **REQUIRED:** Medium to compact image containers:
    * **Catalog Product Thumbnails:** Fixed aspect ratio `3:4` or `1:1`, maximum height capped at `280px - 320px` on desktop and `180px - 220px` on mobile screens.
    * **Homepage Store Gallery:** Uniform Grid (`1:1` square or `16:9` ratio), capped at `300px` height per card.
* **Image Hover Standard (Explicit Standard):**
  * **BANNED:** Desktop cursor hover magnifying zoom (no overlay panel zoom on hover).
  * **APPROVED:** Static high-resolution display with a clean **Click-to-Open Fullscreen Modal Lightbox** (or mobile pinch-to-zoom).

## 3. Dedicated Page Specifications

### PAGE 1: Dedicated Main Homepage (`/`)
* **Purpose:** Establish brand authority, legacy, physical store presence, and company identity.

#### Page Content Structure (Strictly Limited To):
1. **Header:** Brand Logo/Name (Home Trigger), Theme Switcher (Dark/Light), "Explore Collections" CTA button.
2. **Hero Brand Section:**
   * Textile Brand Name & Official Motto/Tagline.
   * High-impact summary of store heritage.
3. **Store Building & Showroom Gallery:**
   * Compact grid gallery displaying physical store exterior architecture, interior showrooms, weaving heritage units, and customer welcome lounge.
4. **About Us & Company History:**
   * Foundational narrative: Year established, founder's vision, craftsmanship, weaver community support, and quality promise.
5. **Primary CTA Banner (Explore Trigger):**
   * Prominent **"Explore Collections"** / **"Discover All Departments"** button leading directly to the Shopping Hub (`/shop`).
6. **Footer:** Contact details, store location address, Google Maps direction link, social links, copyright notice.

---

### PAGE 2: Collections & Shopping Hub (`/shop`)
* **Purpose:** Multi-category family store where customers browse, filter, and buy apparel across all age groups and demographics.

#### Departmental Category Architecture:
1. **Infants & Toddlers (0–2 Years):**
   * Soft organic cotton jhablas, onesies, swaddle cloths, rompers, newborn gift hampers.
2. **Kids Wear - Boys (2–14 Years):**
   * Kurta pyjamas, dhoti sets, festive indo-western, casual shirts, t-shirts, shorts.
3. **Kids Wear - Girls (2–14 Years):**
   * Traditional pattu pavadai, lehenga cholis, party frocks, anarkali suits, casual tops.
4. **Women's Wear:**
   * **Ethnic:** Silk sarees (Kanchipuram, Banarasi, Organza), lehengas, salwar suits, readymade blouses.
   * **Casual/Western:** Kurtis, tops, denims, co-ord sets, nightwear.
5. **Men's Wear:**
   * **Traditional:** Pure silk shirts, dhotis, kurta sets, wedding blazers.
   * **Casual/Formal:** Polo shirts, formal trousers, denim, innerwear.
6. **Home Textiles & Unstitched Materials:**
   * Suitings, shirtings, dress materials, bedsheets, towels, curtains.

#### Interactive Shopping Tools:
* **"Shop by Age & Height" Assistant:** Interactive popup for parents to get precise garment sizing for children.
* **Family Combo Hub:** Dedicated category for Father & Son and Mother & Daughter matching outfit sets.
* **Occasion Filters:** Filter items by Wedding, Party, Festival, or Workwear.
* **Gift Packaging Option:** Checkbox at checkout to add gift wrapping and personalized greeting notes.

---

### PAGE 3: Dedicated Owner Admin Dashboard (`/admin`)
* **Purpose:** A completely isolated, secure management portal for the business owner to control products, stock, and fulfillment without cluttering customer views.

#### Key Admin Functions:
1. **Product & Catalog Management:**
   * Drag-and-drop media uploader for photos and 10-15 second vertical micro-videos.
   * Bulk SKU upload via CSV/Excel template for multi-category items.
   * Multi-attribute assignment: Target Department (Kids/Women/Men), Age Group, Fabric, Price, and GST HSN Codes.
2. **Quality & Safety Badges Toggle:**
   * Checkbox control to display badges: `"100% Skin-Friendly Organic Cotton"`, `"Soft Lining Guarantee"`, `"Silk Mark Certified"`, `"Handloom Authenticity Seal"`.
3. **Stock Control & Order Dispatch:**
   * Variant-level real-time inventory tracking with auto "Out of Stock" triggers.
   * Automated GST invoice creation and single-click courier label printing (Shiprocket, BlueDart, Delhivery, DHL Express).

## 4. Section & Element Alignment Specifications

To address alignment bugs, all code implementations must enforce strict CSS Flexbox/Grid constraints:

### Alignment Rules Matrix:

| Page Section | Alignment Standard | CSS Layout Specification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Header Bar** | Vertical Center Alignment | `display: flex; align-items: center; justify-content: space-between;` |
| **Product Grid** | Strict Uniform Multi-Column Grid | `display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fill, minmax(220px, 1fr)); gap: 20px;` |
| **Product Cards** | Equal Height & Bottom-Aligned CTAs | `display: flex; flex-direction: column; justify-content: space-between; height: 100%;` |
| **Homepage Building Gallery** | Uniform Aspect Ratio Cards | `display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(3, 1fr); gap: 16px; align-items: center;` |
| **Footer Section** | Column Grid with Top Alignment | `display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(4, 1fr); align-items: start; gap: 32px;` |

## 5. Technology Stack & Execution Roadmap

### Platform Recommendations:
1. **Shopify (Recommended):**
   * Custom Theme configured with a standalone landing template (`index.liquid`), distinct shop template (`collection.liquid`), and native Shopify Admin panel for the owner.
2. **WooCommerce / WordPress:**
   * Custom Elementor Pro setup with page rules: Homepage assigned to Static Home, Store assigned to `/shop`, and WordPress Dashboard (`/wp-admin`) for owner management.

## 6. Pre-Launch Master Verification Checklist

- [ ] **Homepage Isolation Check:** Verify that the main homepage contains zero product sale cards and only presents brand info, story, building pictures, and the "Explore" CTA button.
- [ ] **Navigation Link Check:** Ensure clicking the store name or logo from any page immediately returns the user to the Main Homepage (`/`).
- [ ] **Image Size Cap Check:** Confirm product thumbnails are scaled to medium/small sizes (`280px - 320px` max height) across all shopping sub-pages.
- [ ] **Cursor Zoom Disabled:** Confirm hover magnifying glass zoom is removed and replaced with a clean fullscreen modal viewer.
- [ ] **Owner Area Isolation:** Verify that the Owner Dashboard is housed on its own secure, standalone path (`/admin`).
- [ ] **Layout Alignment Check:** Perform visual inspection across desktop and mobile screens to confirm all cards, headers, footers, and grids are aligned without overlapping elements.
